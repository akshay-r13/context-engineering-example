ABOUT SHOPNEST
==================================================

At ShopNest, we are deeply committed to providing our customers with an exceptional shopping experience from the moment they browse our catalogue to the moment their order arrives at their door. We believe that great products are only part of the equation — the service, communication, and trust that surround every transaction are equally important to us.

ShopNest was founded in 2018 with a simple premise: that online shopping should feel personal, reliable, and stress-free. Since then, we have grown to serve customers across all fifty US states, and we continue to invest in the people, technology, and partnerships that allow us to uphold that original promise.

This document outlines our policies on shipping, returns, exchanges, warranties, and customer rights. We encourage all customers to read it in full. If you have questions about anything contained here, our customer service team is available seven days a week via email, phone, and live chat.

==================================================
SECTION 1: ORDER PROCESSING
==================================================

1.1 Order Confirmation
Once you place an order on ShopNest, you will receive an automated confirmation email within five minutes. This email confirms that your order has been received and is being reviewed. Please note that receipt of a confirmation email does not constitute acceptance of your order. ShopNest reserves the right to cancel or refuse any order at any time prior to dispatch for reasons including but not limited to product availability, payment verification failure, or suspected fraudulent activity.

1.2 Processing Times
Orders placed before 2:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST) on a standard business day (Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays) are typically processed the same day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM EST, or on weekends and public holidays, will be processed on the next available business day.

During peak periods — including but not limited to Black Friday, Cyber Monday, holiday seasons, and major promotional events — processing times may extend by one to two additional business days. We endeavour to communicate any such delays proactively via email and on our website.

1.3 Order Amendments and Cancellations
Once an order has been placed and confirmed, amendments or cancellations can only be made within one hour of placing the order, provided that the order has not yet moved to the packing stage. To request an amendment or cancellation, customers must contact our customer service team immediately via phone or live chat. Requests made by email may not be processed in time due to response latency.

If an order has already been packed or dispatched, it cannot be cancelled. Customers wishing to return items after delivery must follow the return process outlined in Section 3 of this document.

1.4 Out-of-Stock Items
In the rare event that an item in your order becomes unavailable after your order is confirmed, we will contact you immediately to offer the following options: (a) a full refund for the unavailable item while fulfilling the remainder of your order, (b) a full cancellation of the order, or (c) a backorder for the item, with an estimated availability date. We will never substitute a product without your explicit consent.

SECTION 2: SHIPPING AND DELIVERY
==================================================

2.1 Shipping Methods
ShopNest currently offers the following shipping methods for domestic US orders:

Standard Shipping: Delivered within 3–7 business days from the date of dispatch. Available on all orders. Free on orders over $75.

Express Shipping: Delivered within 1–2 business days from the date of dispatch. Available on most orders, subject to product size and destination. An additional fee applies.

Overnight Shipping: Delivered the next business day when ordered before 12:00 PM EST. Available in select metropolitan areas only. Subject to carrier availability. An additional fee applies.

Please note that shipping timeframes refer to business days and do not include weekends or public holidays. Timeframes begin from the date of dispatch, not the date the order is placed.

2.2 Dispatch Dates
Your order will be dispatched once it has been packed and collected by the carrier. You will receive a dispatch confirmation email with your tracking number once this occurs. For orders placed with express or overnight shipping, dispatch is prioritised. For standard shipping orders, dispatch typically occurs within one business day of order processing.

2.3 Tracking Your Order
All orders include tracking. Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a tracking number via email. You can use this number to track your parcel directly on the carrier's website (FedEx, UPS, or USPS, depending on the carrier assigned to your order). Please allow up to four hours after receiving your dispatch email for tracking information to become active on the carrier's system.

If you have not received a tracking number within two business days of placing your order, please contact our customer service team.

2.4 Estimated Delivery Timeframes by Region
While we cannot guarantee specific delivery dates, the following are approximate delivery windows from date of dispatch for standard shipping:

- East Coast (NY, MA, FL, GA, etc.): 2–4 business days
- Midwest (IL, OH, MI, MN, etc.): 3–5 business days
- South (TX, LA, TN, NC, etc.): 3–5 business days
- West Coast (CA, WA, OR, etc.): 4–7 business days
- Mountain and Plains (CO, MT, WY, ND, etc.): 4–6 business days
- Hawaii and Alaska: 7–14 business days (standard only; express not available)

These are estimates only and are subject to carrier capacity, weather events, and other factors outside ShopNest's control.

2.5 Delivery Attempts and Failed Deliveries
Our carriers will make up to three delivery attempts for orders requiring a signature. For orders that do not require a signature, the carrier may leave the parcel in a safe location at their discretion (e.g., front porch, mailroom, or with a neighbour). If no safe location is available and a signature is required, a delivery notice will be left and the parcel held at the local carrier facility for up to seven calendar days before it is returned to ShopNest.

If a parcel is returned to us due to failed delivery, we will contact you to arrange re-delivery. A re-delivery fee may apply.

2.6 Delays
ShopNest works hard to ensure orders are delivered within the stated timeframes. However, delays can occur due to circumstances beyond our control, including but not limited to:

- Severe weather events or natural disasters
- Carrier operational disruptions
- National holidays or peak season carrier capacity issues
- Warehouse backlogs during high-demand periods
- Incorrect or incomplete delivery addresses provided at checkout
- Customs processing delays (for international orders)

In the event of a significant delay, we will notify affected customers via email as soon as we are made aware. If your order is delayed, you may contact our customer service team to request a status update at any time.

If your order has not arrived within five business days of the estimated delivery date and no delay notification has been issued, please contact us so we can initiate a carrier investigation. Investigations typically take three to five business days to resolve.

2.7 International Shipping
ShopNest currently ships to Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, and the European Union. International shipping is via express courier only. Delivery timeframes for international orders range from 7–21 business days depending on the destination country and customs processing times. International customers are responsible for any applicable import duties, taxes, and customs fees. These charges are not included in the order total and will be collected by the carrier or customs authority upon delivery.

SECTION 3: RETURNS AND REFUNDS
==================================================

3.1 Return Eligibility
ShopNest accepts returns on most items within 30 calendar days of the delivery date, subject to the conditions below.

To be eligible for a return, items must be:
- In their original, unused condition
- In the original packaging with all tags, accessories, and documentation included
- Not listed under the non-returnable categories in Section 3.2

3.2 Non-Returnable Items
The following categories are not eligible for return under any circumstances:

- Perishable goods or consumables that have been opened
- Personalised or custom-made items
- Digital downloads, software licences, or gift cards once redeemed
- Intimate apparel or swimwear for hygiene reasons
- Hazardous materials or items with flammable components
- Any item that has been used, damaged by the customer, or modified after delivery

3.3 Initiating a Return
To initiate a return, customers must:
1. Log in to their ShopNest account and navigate to Order History.
2. Select the relevant order and click "Return Item(s)."
3. Select the item(s) to return and provide a reason for the return.
4. Download and print the prepaid return label (for eligible returns) or arrange your own return shipping.

Alternatively, customers can contact our customer service team directly to initiate a return by phone, email, or live chat.

Please do not return items without first initiating a return through the above process. Unrequested returns may not be processed.

3.4 Return Shipping
For items returned due to a defect, damage, or an error on ShopNest's part, we will provide a prepaid return shipping label at no cost to the customer.

For returns made for reasons of personal preference (e.g., change of mind, wrong size), the customer is responsible for return shipping costs. In these cases, we recommend using a tracked shipping service, as ShopNest is not responsible for parcels lost in return transit.

3.5 Refund Processing
Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we will notify you by email of the outcome. If the return is approved:

- Refunds will be issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of approval.
- Please note that your bank or payment provider may take an additional 3–5 business days to reflect the credit in your account.
- Original shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to ShopNest's error or a defective item.

3.6 Partial Refunds
Partial refunds may be issued in cases where:
- An item is returned in a condition that differs from its original state (e.g., missing accessories, opened packaging)
- Only part of a bundle or multi-item order is returned
- A promotional discount was applied to the original order

SECTION 4: EXCHANGES
==================================================

4.1 Exchange Policy
ShopNest does not process direct exchanges. If you would like a different size, colour, or product, please return the original item (following the return process in Section 3) and place a new order for the desired item. This ensures the fastest turnaround.

4.2 Defective or Wrong Items
If you receive an item that is defective, damaged, or different from what you ordered, please contact our customer service team within 7 calendar days of delivery. We will arrange a free return and either re-dispatch the correct item or issue a full refund, at your preference.

Please retain the original packaging and do not attempt to use, repair, or modify a defective item before contacting us, as this may affect eligibility for a replacement or refund.

SECTION 5: WARRANTY
==================================================

5.1 Manufacturer Warranty
Many products sold by ShopNest are covered by a manufacturer's warranty. The duration and terms of the warranty vary by product and brand and are listed on the individual product page. Warranty coverage typically applies to defects in materials and workmanship under normal use conditions. It does not cover damage resulting from misuse, accidents, modification, unauthorised repair, or normal wear and tear.

5.2 ShopNest Satisfaction Guarantee
In addition to any applicable manufacturer warranty, ShopNest offers a 30-day satisfaction guarantee on all eligible products. If you are not satisfied with your purchase within the first 30 days, you may return it under our standard return policy for a full refund or store credit.

5.3 Making a Warranty Claim
To make a warranty claim, please contact our customer service team with your order number, a description of the defect, and photographic evidence where applicable. We will assess your claim and liaise with the manufacturer on your behalf where necessary.
